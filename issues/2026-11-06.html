Reranking
Retrieval and knowledge, 4 of 5

"Retrieve 50, rerank, send the top 5."

A second pass that re-scores the top search results with a more accurate but slower model, so the best passages go to the LLM first.

Like a quick sniff of 50 bags, then properly cupping the best five.
