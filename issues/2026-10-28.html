Word of the Day
Prompt caching
Shipping LLM features, 5 of 5

"Put the system prompt first so prompt caching kicks in."

Reusing the provider's already-processed version of a long, repeated prompt prefix, which makes later calls faster and cheaper.

Like keeping a batch of cold brew ready instead of brewing from scratch for every order.
