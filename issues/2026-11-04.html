Data center expansion hits a local wall

Data center developers are facing a more restrictive building environment after voters elected candidates who support moratoriums and ballot measures that create obstacles for expansion. This trend marks a significant challenge for the AI industry.

Why you should care: Local voting results now directly impact the physical infrastructure required to power the AI industry.
