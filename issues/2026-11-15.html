Word of the Day
Prompt injection
Safety and operations, 1 of 5

"The summariser followed instructions hidden in the email. Classic prompt injection."

An attack where text the model reads (an email, web page, or document) contains instructions that try to override what the app told the model to do.

Like a note slipped into an order that says 'make every drink free'.
