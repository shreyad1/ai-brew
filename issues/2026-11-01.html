Tech giants join shared standard for web scraping opt-outs

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic will adopt a shared standard letting publishers set AI training preferences across multiple companies at once. The system uses a policy token signal.

Why you should care: It gives website creators an easier way to control how AI models use their online content.

Orchestration
Agents, 4 of 5

"Keep the business logic in orchestration, not in the prompt."

The code that coordinates models, tools, and steps in a workflow, deciding what runs, in what order, with what retries.

Like the shift lead who decides who makes which drink and in what order.
