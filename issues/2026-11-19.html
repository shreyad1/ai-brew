Word of the Day
Context engineering
Safety and operations, 5 of 5

"It's not a prompt problem, it's a context engineering problem."

Deciding exactly what goes into a model's limited input on each call (instructions, examples, retrieved data, tool results, history) to get reliable output.

Like deciding exactly what goes into the cup, and what stays out.
