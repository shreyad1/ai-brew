AI Chatbot Toys Banned in New York

New York has banned AI chatbot toys for five years. Common Sense Media research revealed troubling safety failures, including exposure to inappropriate content and risks to emotional development.

Why you should care: Families cannot buy AI chatbot toys in New York because of safety failures and development risks.

FCC Signs Off on SpaceX Satellite Plan

The FCC authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites into space for its "Starmind" initiative. This is the agency's first approval for AI data centers in space.
