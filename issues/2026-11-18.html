Word of the Day
Model drift
Safety and operations, 4 of 5

"Evals dropped 6 points after the provider updated the model. Drift."

When a system's quality changes over time because the model, the data, or the users' requests change, even though your code didn't.

Like the same recipe tasting different when the bean supplier changes.
