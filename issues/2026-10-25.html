Pennsylvania requires labels on AI-generated advertisements

Pennsylvania passed a law requiring disclosures on still image, video, audio, or text ads that have been significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence.

Why you should care: You will know when advertisements you see, hear, or read were made or modified by artificial intelligence.

Like a head barista taste-testing every cup, after you've checked their palate agrees with yours.
