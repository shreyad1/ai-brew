Word of the Day
LoRA
Models, speed, and cost, 2 of 5

"We shipped a LoRA adapter instead of a full fine-tune."

Low-Rank Adaptation, a cheap way to fine-tune that trains a small set of extra weights instead of changing the whole model.

Like teaching a new recipe card rather than retraining the barista from scratch.
