The biggest AI news of the day
Pennsylvania will require labels on AI ads

A new Pennsylvania law requires any advertisement that "has been significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence" to include a clear disclosure label.

Why you should care: Readers can know if advertising content they see or hear was created by artificial intelligence.

Evals
Shipping LLM features, 1 of 5

"Did the new prompt regress the evals?"

Automated tests for AI output. A fixed set of inputs, plus checks or graders that score whether the answers are correct, safe, and in the right format.

Like a barista's tasting checklist run on every new recipe before it goes on the menu.
