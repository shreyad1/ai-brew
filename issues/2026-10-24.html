Pennsylvania requires labels on AI-generated ads

Pennsylvania passed a law requiring any advertisement that is significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence to clearly display a label. The rule covers still images, video, audio, and text.

Why you should care: You will soon see clear labels disclosing when political or commercial advertisements use artificial intelligence.
