Word of the Day
Grounding
Shipping LLM features, 3 of 5

"Is that answer grounded, or is the model guessing?"

Making a model answer from specific provided sources (documents, search results, database rows) rather than from what it memorised in training.

Like making a drink from the recipe card in front of you instead of from memory.
