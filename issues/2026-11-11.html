Word of the Day
Quantization
Models, speed, and cost, 3 of 5

"The 4-bit quantized model fits on a single GPU."

Storing a model's numbers at lower precision (e.g. 4-bit instead of 16-bit) so it uses less memory and runs faster, usually with a small quality cost.

Like instant coffee: far lighter to carry, slightly less flavour.
